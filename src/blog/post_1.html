Be Mediocre

The beauty of hand built.

We are always told that you learn more from mistakes than from success. What a shame it will be if the future is a world where humans delegate the ability (or vulnerability) to make mistakes to language models. Language models are trained on the stories (successes and failures) of the human race learning and adapting new technologies. They are then post trained to not make mistakes and then even often prompt engineered (*sigh*): "Do not make mistakes".

Asking a model to not make mistakes is great. It means you are able to get instant gratification of a (often) correct response. However, I would argue the mistakes are where we as humans are able to build unique and wonderful things. There are many huge scientific achievements which were discovered by accident or mistake. This doesn't include the endless list of art that has come about from mistakes.

All we are is stories. The best stories come about from mistakes. If we lose our stories, we lose what makes us interesting. Mistakes, continuous learning and unique experiences are how we stay interesting.

Therefore, the message of this short post is simple.

Be mediocre.

Give yourself the space to be mediocre and learn new things without AI. If AI knows how to do it, chances are a friend, or the internet does too.

I worry that as AI get more widely adopted, we will lose our individuality. Individuality is something AI can (probably) never compete with. Our unique view of the world is adapted over years of unrepeatable experiences. As we increasingly outsource our cognition to a tetrapoloy of huge tech companies, we are in danger of losing our individuality.

There is something truly terrifying about being the most likely response.

One of my (least) favourite concepts is the enshitification of colour. Enshitification of colour is the idea that 40-50 years ago the world was full of a diverse range of colour. In the modern era we all want sleek "modern" designs which just so happen to be colourless and as a result we are progressively living in a more monochromatic world. I fear the same is coming true with AI. The enshitification of cognition. Where we as a population will start to develop a mode collapse as we bias our opinions and thoughts with constant use of AI to do simple tasks like summarisation (do not underestimate the bias that can be brought in by something as simple as summarisation).

AI makes doing incredible things easy. My coach always used to say "why do we do hard things?", the answer: "because no one else will". (He may have been talking about a 90 second plank but the point still stands).
