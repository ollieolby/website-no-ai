Be Mediocre

One of the great tragedies (in my opinion) about the mass adoption of Large Language Models (LLMs) is the aversion to the human quality to be mediocre. Long gone are the days of simple powerpoint templates, crappy home videos and (dare I say it) cognitive thinking. Now you can make something that looks to be modern, proffesional, polished and popular with very little thought. If we outsource all of our disdain in being mediocre to 3 or 4 large tech companies, we are not learning for ourselves how to design unique and valuable contributions to society.

Diversity is the best thing that humans have going for us.

Let me start by scoping my argument down to purely graphic design (but this problem exists everywhere).
